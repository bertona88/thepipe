# Pipe µ requirements

Authority: explicit user direction/adopted decisions → these requirements → selected
embodiment/operation → configuration/code → scoped evidence. Historical files are
not normative. See decisions/PIPE_MICRO_RESET.md.

1. Handle an operation-selected specimen in the initial 300 µm–1 mm domain using
   printed compliant distal structures, remote tendon actuation and external observation.
   Begin with one identified structural resin and native surfaces. Material identity
   is required for fabrication; unknown properties cannot qualify performance.
2. Preserve cylindrical environmental containment, small transfer-chamber interfaces,
   longitudinal z and circumferential θ base positioning. Explicitly represent shared
   versus independent motion, shoulder supports, service limits and physical access.
3. Derive reach from selected shoulder frames and task poses. Enclosure radius is
   independent of distal reach. Camera count, local axes, arm count, transport type,
   environment settings and all loads remain embodiment decisions.
4. Separate physical supports, observed evidence and control responsibility. Only
   fresh identified observations of required degrees of freedom admit control.
   Record covariance, calibration/source identity and invalidity. Never substitute truth.
5. Execute pickup, transfer, dual support, donor unloading, observed separation,
   receiver retention, withdrawal and independent destination inspection. Jaw opening,
   apparent co-location and logical ownership are insufficient outcomes.
6. Guard tool/palm/finger/flexure/support/service/part/fixture motion and intended
   contact. Unknown or blocked motion refuses. State-dependent recovery must execute
   and be recorded; loss of support cannot become success. Hardware emergency stops,
   energy/current/tension bounds, travel stops and enclosure protection require testing.
7. Every numerical assumption has units, source, status and a valid operation domain.
   Geometry-only, synthetic contact, calibrated and hardware evidence cannot inherit
   each other's claims. Old optical accuracy targets are ambitions, not new qualification.
8. Record fixed ticks, revision and source/configuration hashes, environment and separate
   release/separation/retention evidence IDs. Replays and CAD use authoritative records;
   missing or corrupt data yields unavailable. No legacy defaults or schema fallbacks.
9. Physical qualification requires recorded specimens/process, repeated outcomes,
   independently measured final pose/separation and predeclared uncertainty/acceptance
   limits. Repository cutover does not establish physical feasibility or reliability.

The active operation contract sets synthetic bounds only. Remaining physical evidence
is explicitly absent in IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md.
