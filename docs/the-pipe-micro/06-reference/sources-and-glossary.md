# Sources and glossary

## Primary design source

The user-supplied discussion and current request, documented on 12 September 2026, are authoritative for the desired direction and preferences. This is a synthesis of those ideas with engineering elaboration. It is not an exhaustive literature review or a report of new experiments.

The supplied conversation included many scientific links. The compact selection below records relevant precedents and their limits. Available abstract/indexed descriptions were checked during preparation; full-text experimental methods were not reviewed here. No universal resin property or machine performance number is derived from them.

## Selected research precedents

| Topic | Source | What it supports here | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Printed compliant grasper with sensing | Power and colleagues, 2018, [A Monolithic Force-Sensitive 3D Microgripper Fabricated on the Tip of an Optical Fiber Using 2-Photon Polymerization](https://doi.org/10.1002/smll.201703964) | A tethered compliant microgripper with integrated force sensing has a 2PP fabrication precedent | It does not qualify this tendon-driven 10–15 mm arm or camera-read force design |

| Humidity and polymer surface charge | Hori, 2000, [The lateral migration of surface charges on poly(methyl methacrylate)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304388699000595) | PMMA film charge spreading/decay was observed to depend on humidity in the reported conditions | It supplies no operating RH recipe or charge-decay time for an unspecified printed resin |
| Printed microfluidic integration | Mu and colleagues, 2023 preprint, [Implantable Photonic Neural Probes with 3D-Printed Microfluidics and Applications to Uncaging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.10387) | 2PP microfluidic structures can be integrated with an existing small device | This is a different device; a moving, sealed flexure lumen remains unqualified |

The scaling, beam, ideal suction and stereo relations in the atlas are explicitly idealised engineering relations. Their assumptions are stated where used. Numerical examples are calculations from declared inputs, not reported measurements. More specialised contact behaviour is framed as a hypothesis or a bounded research precedent.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this atlas |
| --- | --- |
| 2PP | Two-photon polymerization; the fine polymer fabrication method central to this concept |
| 2GL | A fabrication capability mentioned by the user; no exact process definition or guaranteed surface finish was provided, so this atlas does not expand it into a specification |
| MicroSLA | A microscale stereolithography process category; capabilities depend on the selected system and material |
| Distal | Near the tool and workpiece, away from the external actuator |
| Flexure | A mechanism region whose elastic deformation provides useful motion |
| Tendon | A tensile transmission element that pulls a mechanism |
| Preload | Intentional initial loading that maintains engagement or a chosen mechanical state |
| Contact microarchitecture | Deliberately designed contact geometry, compliance and surrounding fluid/debris paths |
| Pull-off | Force response associated with separating an adhesive contact under specified conditions |
| Peel | Progressive separation through a changing contact boundary or angle |
| Stiction | Observed resistance to initiating motion or separation, potentially from several coupled effects |
| RH | Relative humidity, interpreted together with temperature |
| Load-lock | A transfer compartment that reduces disturbance to the main chamber; vacuum operation is not implied here |
| Retention | Physical support adequate for a specified operation and disturbance envelope |
| Ownership | Logical responsibility for coordinated control; it does not replace the contact/support state |
| Verified release | Evidence that the destination retains the part and the donor has separated, within declared measurement capability |
| Metrology | Calibrated measurement with stated uncertainty, rather than visibility or pixel sampling alone |
| Native surface | The surface resulting from the recorded fabrication, cleaning and cure process, without an added functional treatment |
| Candidate | A possible implementation not selected or qualified by this atlas |
