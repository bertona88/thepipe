# Transport and shoulder support

**Chosen capability:** longitudinal z and circumferential θ positioning. **Open mechanism:** how the base is constrained, driven and supported.

The transport system brings a small local manipulator to useful locations. It also carries tendon reactions, hose forces, shoulder moments and contact loads. The tiny arm reduces some loads, especially gravity and inertia, but preload and manipulation forces can dominate the resulting design.

## Separate guidance from actuation

A cable applies a force along a line. Two commanded cable lengths do not automatically constrain all unwanted carriage translations and rotations. A practical base also needs a defined support: guide surfaces, a ring, a shoe with geometric constraint, a flexure stage or another kinematic arrangement.

The appealing polymer-shoe concept is a candidate: a lightly preloaded printed carriage is constrained against a suitable cylindrical guide, while remote tendon loops command motion. Contact friction, wear particles, stick-slip and lift-off under tendon reaction must then be understood. Merely pressing a pad against a smooth cylinder is not a complete two-axis mechanism.

## The shoulder can be inside the enclosure radius

An outer transport carriage may carry an inward extension; an internal support can have a smaller radius; or the work can take place near the wall. Any extension belongs to the load path and optical scene. Its deflection is observed and bounded rather than omitted.

## What useful transport performance means

Useful travel brings the shoulder to a region that the distal arm can correct within. During a fine operation the base must remain stable enough, or move predictably enough, for observed control. A parking or holding feature may be valuable even if it is not a precision detent.

Travel limits, possible passage between carriages, service wrap and fallback parking locations define real capability. The base is not required to provide micron-class open-loop positioning, but it must allow repeatable acquisition of the local workspace and safe contact.
