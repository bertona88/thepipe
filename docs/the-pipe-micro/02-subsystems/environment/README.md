# A controlled assembly chamber

**Chosen direction:** enclose the working region and use small transfer chambers for incoming objects and tools. The intended character is a microscopic clean workshop with measurable environmental conditions.

The chamber is part of the manipulation system because humidity, contamination, temperature, airflow and charge history influence the interface. It is useful even if the first experiments use ordinary filtered air and modest instrumentation.

## Functional regions

The working region needs stable observation and low disturbance. Conditioning flow can enter through a region that reduces direct jets at the parts. An outlet gives a defined circulation path. Part and tool locks reduce the need to expose the entire volume during loading. External service equipment keeps pumps, valves and motor heat away from the immediate scene where practical.

Sealed does not necessarily mean hermetic. The intended concept includes controlled gas exchange and service feedthroughs. A small enclosure may reduce the gas volume to condition, while polymer moisture uptake and surface equilibration can still make some transitions slow.

## Useful observed conditions

Temperature and RH describe part of the gas state. Pressure and flow help explain disturbances and vacuum-tool conditions. A record of loading, cleaning and contact cycles helps interpret contamination. Optical inspections can identify some particles and residue. Surface charge requires a suitable measurement or empirically established proxy if a quantitative charge claim is needed.

No cleanroom class, RH setpoint or filtration specification is selected here. “Micro cleanroom” describes the functional intent; verified cleanliness is an experimental result.

## Material boundaries

A polymer-only distal structure is compatible with external metal-containing motors and equipment. An insulating arm is not automatically electrically neutral. External ion conditioning is a possible later option, but its effectiveness inside the chamber and any added airflow or chemical effects would need evaluation.
