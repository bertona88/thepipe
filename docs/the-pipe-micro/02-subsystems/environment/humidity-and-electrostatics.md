# Humidity and electrostatics

The user's proposal is to use a controllable chamber, potentially including humidity cycles, to manage electrostatic effects while keeping the distal system free of metal. This remains an active idea in the architecture.

## Why material and history matter

Contact, sliding and separation between insulating materials can leave charge. A metal-free construction removes common conductive paths but does not stop charge generation. Texture may change the amount and geometry of contact; it is not a universal neutralisation method.

PMMA literature is relevant background, but a cured crosslinked 2PP methacrylate resin is not simply bulk PMMA. Published PMMA film observations show humidity-dependent charge spreading and decay under their experimental conditions. They do not establish a usable RH setpoint or discharge time for this arm. The [research notes](../../06-reference/sources-and-glossary.md) retain that boundary.

## Steady humidity and cycling are two different ideas

A stable RH setting seeks repeatable contact behaviour during assembly. A conditioning cycle deliberately changes the environment to alter surface state, then returns to an operating condition. Both can be explored with the same chamber, but neither can be selected from a generic claim that “more humidity removes static.”

Possible conditioning-cycle states include retained parts, changed RH, a dwell determined by measurement, return toward the assembly condition and re-establishment of suitable observation/contact behaviour. These are conceptual states, not a prescribed humidity recipe.

## Coupled effects

More moisture may alter conductivity and charge relaxation, but can also alter capillary adhesion, polymer dimensions or stiffness, and optical surfaces. Local cold surfaces can condense water even when a gas sensor elsewhere looks acceptable. RH depends on temperature, so RH and temperature records belong together.

A humidity excursion can change later dry-contact behaviour through hysteresis or residues. “Back at the original RH” does not necessarily mean “back at the original interface state.”

## What would make the idea useful

Useful evidence is a repeatable improvement in the actual operation: less uncontrolled attraction or rotation, predictable pull-off and reliable placement under known conditions. If charge is measured, decay time and its variability should be tied to the particular resin, contact pair and history. If it is not measured, describe improved manipulation behaviour rather than claiming complete discharge.

The architecture should preserve humidity cycles as an option while allowing a simple, stable environment to be the first operating mode.
